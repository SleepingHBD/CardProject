First-draft rules

How Prowlbound works

  1. Your uploaded pet is the champion card and stays with you throughout the expedition.
  2. In battle, draw five techniques and spend three Energy each turn. The enemy always shows its next move.
  3. Elemental Edge adds +2 damage: Ember beats Gust, Gust beats Tide, and Tide beats Ember.
  4. Health carries between encounters. Add cards, gain talents, find relics and choose safer or riskier paths.
  5. Defeat the final boss to finish a run. Win or lose, your pet earns permanent Bond XP.

Privacy: your pet image is stored only in this browser's local storage.
