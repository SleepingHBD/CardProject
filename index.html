      THE WHISKERKEEP RULEBOOK

      How to Play Project: Prowl

      The complete rules of an elemental duel, from reading your first card to claiming the sixth trophy.

      🏆

      Victory at a glance

      Collect two Fire, two Gust, and two Water trophies before your opponent.

      🔥2 Fire 🍃2 Gust 💧2 Water

      The Rounds Won display records your victories, but it does not complete the match. Your six elemental trophy slots do.

      1

      Objective and match structure

      • A match is a series of rounds against your opponent.
      • Winning a round awards exactly one trophy. A drawn round awards none.
      • Win the match by collecting two Fire, two Gust, and two Water trophies.
      • The Rounds Won score is a match record only. It is not a separate win condition.
      2

      Deck, hand, and difficulty

      The shared draw pile contains 39 cards: two copies of every Common and Uncommon card, and one copy of every Rare, Epic, and Legendary card. Both duelists draw up to six cards.

      Guided
      Shows every planned card's element and power range.
      Instinct
      Hides the opponent's current cards and formation size, but reveals one motive, one formation habit, and one commitment habit that persist for the match.
      Blind
      Hides the opponent's current cards, formation size, and habits. Infer their persistent habits from Previous Rounds History.

      Every difficulty includes Previous Rounds History, which records both revealed formations, lane results, Round Points, trophy context, and the claimed trophy after a round is complete. Habits are tendencies, not guarantees. Difficulty changes available information, never the scoring rules or card strength.

      3

      Read every part of a card

      PowerThe printed number and starting value of its Clash Total.

      ElementFire, Gust, or Water. It determines Element Edge and the trophy's element.

      Formation RoleVanguard, Link, or Finisher. Meeting its condition adds Role +1.

      RarityCommon through Legendary. It changes the card frame and how many copies enter the shared deck, but never the clash rules or scoring.

      Move and loreCharacter flavor only. Cards have no unlisted abilities or hidden effects.

      🔥 Firebeats 🍃 Gustbeats 💧 Waterbeats 🔥 Fire
      4

      Play a round from start to finish

      1. Read. Study the live clues, visible habits, or Previous Rounds History available in the chosen difficulty.
      2. Build. Drag or click 1–3 cards into consecutive lanes. The first card enters Lane 1.
      3. Order. Return cards to your hand or rearrange them until the formation works as intended.
      4. Preview. Review known bonuses and totals. Hidden information remains unknown until the clash.
      5. Commit. Lock the formation. Every committed card is spent this round.
      6. Resolve. Score each contested lane, then calculate Round Points for the whole formation.
      7. Claim and review. Award a trophy if someone won, inspect the aftermath, then press Next Round.

      Lane matching is direct: Lane 1 fights Lane 1, Lane 2 fights Lane 2, and Lane 3 fights Lane 3. This is not one combined 3v3 battle.

      5

      Calculate a card's Clash Total

      Power + Element Edge + Role = Clash Total

      PowerBegin with the printed number.

      Element Edge +2Add 2 when the card's element beats the opposing card's element.

      Role +1Add 1 when the card meets its Formation Role condition.

      Lane resultHigher Clash Total wins. Equal Clash Totals draw that lane.

      Element Edge is a bonus, not an automatic win. A high-Power card can still defeat a card that has Element Edge.

      6

      Formation Roles

      Vanguard +1

      Activate it by committing the Vanguard in Lane 1.

      Link +1

      Activate it in Lane 2 or 3 when the card committed directly before the Link has a different element from the Link card.

      Finisher +1

      Activate it by committing the Finisher as the final card in a 2–3 card formation.

      Roles never restrict placement. You may commit any card in any available lane. If its Role condition is not met, the card still clashes normally but receives no Role +1.

      7

      Calculate Round Points

      Clash Total decides each contested lane. Round Points decide who wins the round.

      Number of won lanes× 2 Round Points + Number of extra cards× 1 Round Point = Round Points
      1. Resolve contested lanes A contested lane has one card from each side. Each won lane earns 2 Round Points. Drawn and lost lanes earn 0.
      2. Count extra cards An extra card is a committed card with no opposing card in its lane. Each extra card adds 1 Round Point.
      3. Compare the totals Add all Round Points. More Round Points wins the round. Equal Round Points produce a draw and no trophy.

      Common outcomes

      1 card vs 1 card 2–0 · Win

      Your card wins the only contested lane. There are no extra cards.

      1 card vs 2 cards 2–1 · Win

      Your card wins its lane for 2. The opponent's extra card adds 1.

      1 card vs 3 cards 2–2 · Draw

      Your card wins its lane for 2. The opponent's two extra cards also add 2.

      2 cards vs 1 card 1–0 · Win

      The contested lane draws. Your extra card adds the round's only point.

      Keep the two scores separate: an extra card adds 1 Round Point, but it never clashes and never changes Power, Element Edge, Role, or Clash Total.

      8

      Award the trophy

      • If your won lanes decide the round, claim one of your lane-winning cards.
      • If you have several lane-winning cards, you choose which becomes the trophy. If there is only one, it is claimed automatically.
      • If points from extra cards decide the round, your first extra card becomes the trophy automatically.
      • A drawn round awards no trophy.
      9

      Discard, draw, and continue

      • The trophy leaves the deck and enters the winner's collection.
      • Every other committed card enters the discard pile.
      • Unplayed cards remain in hand.
      • Each hand refills toward six cards before the next round.
      • When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled back into it.
      10

      End the match

      Required collection2 Fire + 2 Gust + 2 Water trophies.

      Extra trophiesA third trophy of one element is allowed, but it cannot replace a missing element.

      OpponentThey win under exactly the same trophy condition.

      Rounds WonThis display tracks round results only and never replaces the trophy goal.

      11

      Subtle strategy

      Choose size with a purpose.One card conserves resources. More cards cover more lanes and may score as extra cards, but every committed card is spent.

      Order before committing.Formation order determines lanes and can activate or disable Vanguard, Link, and Finisher bonuses.

      Plan the trophy element.When several cards win lanes, arrange to give yourself a useful trophy choice. For an extra-card win, the first extra card is fixed.

      Respect printed Power.Element Edge helps, but a two-point bonus does not always overcome a much stronger card.

      Read hidden information.In Guided, use the live plan clues. In Instinct, combine all three visible habits. In Blind, compare several entries in Previous Rounds History to infer those hidden habits yourself.

      Track the real goal.Do not spend valuable cards collecting a third trophy of an element when another element is still missing.

      SUPER SECRET STUFF WIP

      Mode Rules

      Choose your lanes

      Commit 1–4 cards in any of the four lanes, one card per lane. Gaps are allowed. Lane 1 faces Lane 1, Lane 2 faces Lane 2, and so on. Your lane positions—not the order you click cards—determine who fights and which role bonuses activate. Every role can be placed in any lane.

      Win lanes to earn trophies

      Clash Total = printed Power + Element Edge + your card's role bonus + received Rally bonus. The higher total wins the lane; equal totals draw.

      Fire beats Gust, Gust beats Water, and Water beats Fire. The element with the advantage gains +2 Power.

      A lane win earns 2 trophies of the winning card's element. A drawn or lost lane earns 0.

      Both sides keep the trophies they earn. There is no overall round winner or trophy choice. For example, if your Fire card wins Lane 1 and the opponent's Water card wins Lane 2, you earn 2 Fire trophies and the opponent earns 2 Water trophies.

      Unlock unopposed trophies

      An unopposed card has no opposing card in its lane. Each lane you win unlocks one of your unopposed cards to earn 1 trophy of its own element, up to two unopposed trophies per round. One lane win unlocks one card, not every unopposed card.

      Rewards go from Lane 1 to Lane 4, skipping unopposed cards whose element already has 6 trophies. A lane win still unlocks a reward even when the winning card's element is complete.

      No lane wins means no unopposed trophies. Drawn lanes do not unlock rewards. Avoiding every battle earns nothing. Power and role bonuses never increase an unopposed card's 1 trophy.

      Four formation roles

      • Vanguard: +1 when committed in Lane 1.
      • Link: +1 when the card in the neighbouring lane to its left has a different element. An empty lane to its left gives no bonus.
      • Finisher: +1 when it is your rightmost card and you commit at least two cards. It does not have to occupy Lane 4. A lone Finisher gets no bonus.
      • Rally: Gives +1 Power to the card in the neighbouring lane to its left—not itself. For example, Rally in Lane 3 boosts Lane 2, but cannot reach Lane 1 if Lane 2 is empty. Rally in Lane 1 gives no bonus. The receiving card can also earn its own role bonus. Each Rally gives only +1, even if it receives a boost.

      Collect trophies of all three elements

      Collect 6 Fire, 6 Gust and 6 Water trophies before the opponent. Each element stops at 6 trophies: extra Fire trophies cannot replace missing Water or Gust trophies. Cards from completed elements can still win lanes, deny opposing trophies and unlock unopposed cards.

      Finish resolving every lane before checking the winner. If both players collect all required trophies in the same round, the duel is a draw.

      Unlike Normal Play, trophies are counters, not claimed cards. They are awarded automatically for individual lanes. You do not choose a card as a trophy; all committed cards go to their owner's discard pile and stay in that owner's deck cycle.

      Manage your own hand

      Both players start with 7 cards. Between rounds, each draws up to 3 without exceeding 7 cards in hand.

      Commit 3 cards to maintain your hand size, or 4 to spend 1 card from your reserves. Commit 1 or 2 to rebuild your hand by up to 2 or 1 cards, subject to the 7-card limit.

      You and your opponent have separate decks and discard piles. All committed cards go into their owner's discard pile, including winning cards. Only that owner's empty draw pile can trigger a reshuffle. All 24 cards stay in circulation.

      Build your deck

      Each deck contains 24 cards and costs no more than 120. Include at least 4 cards of each element. You may use up to 2 copies of each Common or Uncommon card, and 1 of each Rare, Epic or Legendary. No role is required.

      Deck cost is Power plus a rarity surcharge: Common/Uncommon +0, Rare +1, Epic +4, Legendary +6. It never changes combat Power. Choose a starter deck or build and save your own before the duel. Every rival follows these same deck limits.

      Choose a rival

      Confirm your deck to open the rival page. Versatile Duelist uses Balanced Formation. Banner Captain uses Rally Company. Cycle Weaver uses Element Weavers. Twilight Duelist uses Last Light. Choose one or let Random Rival choose for you. Use Back to Your Deck to change your deck before starting.

      Each rival draws three habits from a pool that suits its deck. They stay fixed throughout the duel. Restarting or changing difficulty retains the rival, deck and habits; start again from the lobby to roll new habits or a new random rival. Rivals adapt to completed rounds, not your hidden hand or deck.

      Read your opponent

      Guided shows the opponent's elements and Power ranges. Instinct conceals their formation but reveals three persistent habits. Blind conceals formation, habits and the in-game rival identity. Select Random Rival if you also want to deduce the deck style yourself. Previous Rounds History records all four lanes after a round is complete.

      Keep the duel moving

      After four consecutive rounds in which neither side earns trophies, a reminder encourages you to seek battles. If neither side finishes its trophies within 150 rounds, the duel ends in a draw.
