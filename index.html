THE GUILD CODEX

Laws of the Duel

1

Power: Add all bonuses to the number on your card. The higher total wins the clash.

2

Focus: Every clashing card gets +2 when you commit one card, +1 for two cards, and no bonus for three.

3

Chain: A card gets +1 when its element differs from your previous card. The first lane cannot Chain.

4

Element Edge: Gain +2 when your element beats the opposing one: Ember beats Gust, Gust beats Tide, and Tide beats Ember.

5

Pressure: If clash wins are tied, the side that committed more cards wins the round. Every committed card is spent.
