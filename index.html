Power
The number printed on the card.
Edge +2
Your committed card's element counters the opposing element.
Lane win +2
Every paired lane you win adds 2 Formation Points.
Role +1
Earned when that card's exact Formation Role condition is met.
  • Vanguard +1 when committed to Lane 1.
  • Link +1 in Lane 2 or 3 when the card committed directly before the Link has a different element from the Link card.
  • Finisher +1 when it is the final card you commit in a two- or three-card formation and it faces an opposing card.

Formation Roles do not restrict placement. Any card may be committed in any available lane. If its condition is not met, it still clashes normally but receives no Role +1.

THE DUEL CODEX

Laws of the Duel

1

Goal: A round win turns one of your played cards into a trophy. Collect two Ember, two Gust, and two Tide trophies.

2

Commitment Phase: Choose one, two, or three cards before Professor Paws reveals his plan. Your chosen size locks for the round.

3

Power: Add active bonuses. Element Edge gives +2: Ember beats Gust, Gust beats Tide, and Tide beats Ember.

4

Formation Score: A won paired lane adds 2 points. Every extra, unpaired card adds 1 Pressure point.

5

Formation Role placement: Roles never restrict placement. An unmet Role simply gives no Role +1.

6

Vanguard +1: Activates when the Vanguard is committed in Lane 1.

7

Link +1: Activates in Lane 2 or Lane 3 when the card committed directly before the Link has a different element from the Link card.

8

Finisher +1: Activates when the Finisher is your last committed card in a two- or three-card formation and faces an opposing card.

9

Pressure: Extra cards do not clash; each adds 1 Formation Point. Highest Formation Score wins, and equal scores draw.

10

Reshuffle: Non-trophy cards enter the discard pile and return when the draw pile runs out.

THE WHISKERKEEP RULEBOOK

How to Play Project: Prowl

The complete laws of an elemental duel, from your opening hand to the final trophy.

🏆

Victory at a glance

Collect two Ember, two Gust, and two Tide trophies before Professor Paws.

🔥2 Ember 🍃2 Gust 💧2 Tide

The round score records your victories, but it does not complete the match. Your six elemental trophy slots do.

1

Match setup

  • The shared draw pile starts with 36 cards: two copies of every card in the archive.
  • You and Professor Paws each draw up to six cards.
  • You and Professor Paws independently choose a one-, two-, or three-card commitment.
  • Your chosen size locks before the professor's plan or commitment is revealed.
  • His commitment becomes visible in Guided, Veiled, and Blind, but remains concealed in Instinct.
2

Difficulty and scouting

Guided
Shows every planned card's element and power range.
Veiled
Shows either the element or power range of each card.
Blind
Reveals the locked commitment count but seals all individual card clues.
Instinct
Conceals the commitment and Pressure while revealing one motive, one formation habit, and one commitment habit. Habits persist for the duel and are tendencies, not guarantees.

Difficulty changes the information you receive, not the scoring rules or Professor Paws' card strength.

3

PowerThe card's starting clash score.

Formation RoleShows how to earn the card's Role +1 placement bonus.

RarityChanges the outer frame, but gives no automatic combat bonus.

Move and loreGive the character personality; they do not add hidden effects.

4

How to play a round

  1. Declare. Choose one, two, or three cards during the Commitment Phase. Both sides lock their size independently.
  2. Scout. After locking, read the professor's revealed commitment and card clues—or, in Instinct, use his three known habits.
  3. Build. Drag or click exactly the number of cards you declared into your lanes. You may return or reorder them before committing.
  4. Preview. Check the displayed bonus and forecast for each paired lane.
  5. Commit. Lock your formation. Every committed card will be spent after the round.
  6. Clash. Paired cards resolve from Lane 1 to Lane 3. Every won lane adds 2 Formation Points; every extra card adds 1 Pressure point.
  7. Claim. After a normal win with multiple winning lanes, choose which winning card becomes your trophy. Pressure trophies remain automatic.
  8. Review. Study every total and trophy result, then press Next Round.
5

Clash scoring

Power + Element Edge + Role = Clash Total

  • Element Edge +2: awarded when your element beats the opposing element.
  • Vanguard +1: Play it in Lane 1.
  • Link +1: A Link can activate in Lane 2 or Lane 3. It gains +1 when the card committed directly before the Link has a different element from the Link card itself.
  • Finisher +1: Play it last in a two- or three-card formation. It must face an opposing card.

Formation Roles do not restrict placement. You may commit any card in any available lane. If its Role condition is not met, the card still clashes normally but receives no Role +1.

The higher total wins the lane. Equal totals draw that lane. Element Edge is helpful, but never an automatic win.

6

Winning the round

  • Every paired lane victory adds 2 Formation Points; drawn lanes add nothing.
  • Every extra, unpaired card adds 1 Pressure point.
  • Add lane points and Pressure. The higher Formation Score wins the round.
  • If Formation Scores are equal, the round draws and awards no trophy.

One card can beat two. A lane victory scores 2 points against one extra card's 1 Pressure point.

One card cannot beat three through only one lane. Its 2 lane points meet 2 Pressure points, producing a round draw.

7

Understanding Pressure

Pressure converts every extra, unpaired committed card into 1 Formation Point. Pressure never changes an individual card's Clash Total.

How it scoresOne extra card adds 1 Pressure point; two extra cards add 2.

What it rewardsIf Pressure produces the winning score, the first extra card becomes the trophy.

What it cannot doPressure does not alter a paired card's Power, Edge, Role, or Clash Total.

What it costsEvery extra card is spent after the round.

Example: You commit two cards and Professor Paws commits one. The paired lane draws for 0 points. Your extra card supplies 1 Pressure point, so you win the Formation Score 1–0.

8

Trophies and card flow

  • On a normal round win, choose one of your lane-winning cards as the trophy. If only one card won a lane, it is claimed automatically.
  • On a Pressure win, your first extra, unpaired card becomes your trophy.
  • A trophy leaves circulation and fills its matching elemental slot.
  • All other committed cards enter the discard pile; unplayed cards remain in hand.
  • Hands refill toward six cards. When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled back in.
9

Complete win conditions

You win immediately after a round in which your collection contains at least:

  • Two Ember trophies
  • Two Gust trophies
  • Two Tide trophies

Extra trophies of one element do not replace a missing element. Professor Paws wins under the same condition.

10

Subtle strategy

Plan the trophy.Win with more than one element to choose the trophy afterward. For Pressure, make the needed element your first extra card.

Read the commitment tradeoff.One card is efficient, two are reliable, and three create the most Pressure and lane coverage.

Activate Formation Roles.Start with Vanguard, place Link after a card whose element differs from the Link card, and end a longer play with Finisher.

Risk the Finisher.Finisher gains +1 only when an opposing card is there to clash with it.

Read the range.Against a hidden exact power, compare your lowest and highest possible totals before risking a valuable card.

Avoid empty progress.A third trophy of one element is allowed, but it does not fill one of your remaining elemental slots.

Declare deliberately.Lock your size before seeing the plan. Consider your hand, trophy needs, and Professor Paws' known habits.
