THE DUEL CODEX

Laws of the Duel

1

Goal: A round win turns one of your played cards into a trophy. Collect two Ember, two Gust, and two Tide trophies.

2

Power: Add bonuses to your card's power. Element Edge gives +2: Ember beats Gust, Gust beats Tide, and Tide beats Ember.

3

Focus: Each clashing card gets +2 when you commit one card, +1 for two cards, and no bonus for three.

4

Tactics give +1: Put Vanguard in Lane 1. Put Link after a different element. Make Finisher your last card in a two- or three-card play; it must face a card.

5

Overwhelm: When three cards face one, the three-card formation gains +2 in Lane 1.

6

Pressure: If clash wins are tied, the side that committed more cards wins the round. Every committed card is spent.

7

Reshuffle: Non-trophy cards enter the discard pile and return when the draw pile runs out.

THE WHISKERKEEP RULEBOOK

How to Play Project: Prowl

The complete laws of an elemental duel, from your opening hand to the final trophy.

🏆

Victory at a glance

Collect two Ember, two Gust, and two Tide trophies before Professor Paws.

🔥2 Ember 🍃2 Gust 💧2 Tide

The round score records your victories, but it does not complete the match. Your six elemental trophy slots do.

1

Match setup

  • The shared draw pile starts with 36 cards: two copies of every card in the archive.
  • You and Professor Paws each draw up to six cards.
  • The professor secretly commits one to three cards before you build your formation.
  • His commitment count and formation bonus—Focus or Overwhelm—are visible in Guided, Veiled, and Blind, but concealed in Instinct.
2

Difficulty and scouting

Guided
Shows every planned card's element and power range.
Veiled
Shows either the element or power range of each card.
Blind
Seals all individual card clues but still shows the commitment count and formation bonus.
Instinct
Also conceals commitment and formation bonus, but reveals one motive, one formation habit, and one commitment habit. Habits persist for the duel and are tendencies, not guarantees.

Difficulty changes the information you receive, not the scoring rules or Professor Paws' card strength.

3

4

How to play a round

  1. Scout. Read the professor's commitment, formation bonus, and card clues—or, in Instinct, use the three known habits instead.
  2. Build. Drag or click one to three cards into your lanes. You may return or reorder them before committing.
  3. Preview. Check the displayed bonus and forecast for each paired lane.
  4. Commit. Lock your formation. Every committed card will be spent after the round.
  5. Clash. Paired cards resolve from Lane 1 to Lane 3. Three cards Overwhelm one; extra, unpaired cards become Pressure.
  6. Claim. After a normal win with multiple winning lanes, choose which winning card becomes your trophy. Pressure trophies remain automatic.
  7. Review. Study every total and trophy result, then press Next Round.
5

Clash scoring

Power + Edge + Focus + Tactic + Overwhelm = Total

  • Element Edge +2: awarded when your element beats the opposing element.
  • Focus: +2 per clashing card when committing one card, +1 for two, and +0 for three.
  • Vanguard +1: Play it in Lane 1.
  • Link +1: Play it immediately after a different element.
  • Finisher +1: Play it last in a two- or three-card formation. It must face an opposing card.
  • Overwhelm +2: awarded in Lane 1 when your three-card formation faces exactly one opposing card.

The higher total wins the lane. Equal totals draw that lane. Element Edge is helpful, but never an automatic win.

6

Winning the round

  • Count the lanes won by each side; drawn lanes count for neither player.
  • The side with more lane wins takes the round.
  • If lane wins are tied, the side that committed more cards wins through Pressure.
  • If both lane wins and commitments are tied, the round is a draw and awards no trophy.

Pressure cannot overturn a normal loss. A 0–1 result remains a loss even if you committed more cards.

Overwhelm acts before Pressure. In a three-versus-one round, +2 changes the Lane 1 clash total; Pressure only breaks a tied lane score afterward.

7

Understanding Pressure

Pressure is a formation tie-breaker created by committing more cards than your opponent. Only paired cards clash; any card beyond the opponent's formation is an extra Pressure card.

When it activatesPressure matters only when both sides win the same number of clashing lanes.

What it rewardsThe larger formation wins the round, and its first extra card becomes the trophy.

What it cannot doPressure cannot reverse an unequal lane score. If you lose the clashes 0–1, you still lose the round.

What it costsEvery extra card is spent, and committing more cards lowers Focus on all of your clashing cards.

Example: You commit two cards and Professor Paws commits one. The only clash draws, making the lane score 0–0. Your larger formation wins through Pressure, and your second card becomes the trophy.

8

Trophies and card flow

  • On a normal round win, choose one of your lane-winning cards as the trophy. If only one card won a lane, it is claimed automatically.
  • On a Pressure win, your first extra, unpaired card becomes your trophy.
  • A trophy leaves circulation and fills its matching elemental slot.
  • All other committed cards enter the discard pile; unplayed cards remain in hand.
  • Hands refill toward six cards. When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled back in.
9

Complete win conditions

You win immediately after a round in which your collection contains at least:

  • Two Ember trophies
  • Two Gust trophies
  • Two Tide trophies

Extra trophies of one element do not replace a missing element. Professor Paws wins under the same condition.

10

Subtle strategy

Plan the trophy.Win with more than one element to choose the trophy afterward. For Pressure, make the needed element your first extra card.

Read the commitment triangle.One card has strong Focus against two; two keeps Focus against three; three gains Overwhelm against one.

Activate Tactics.Start with Vanguard, follow a different element with Link, and end a longer play with Finisher.

Risk the Finisher.Finisher gains +1 only when an opposing card is there to clash with it.

Read the range.Against a hidden exact power, compare your lowest and highest possible totals before risking a valuable card.

Avoid empty progress.A third trophy of one element is allowed, but it does not fill one of your remaining elemental slots.

Spend three cards deliberately.Use three to punish a likely lone card with Overwhelm or to threaten Pressure, but remember that all three cards are spent.
