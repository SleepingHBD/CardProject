THE DUEL CODEX

Quick Duel Reference

Use this for reminders during play. Open the Rulebook for complete explanations and examples.

1

Build

Commit 1–3 cards in order. They fill Lane 1, then Lane 2, then Lane 3. You may reorder them before committing.

2

Clash by lane

Lane 1 fights Lane 1, Lane 2 fights Lane 2, and Lane 3 fights Lane 3. Cards never combine into one formation total.

3

Calculate Clash Total

Power + Element Edge + Role = Clash Total

🔥 Emberbeats 🍃 Gustbeats 💧 Tidebeats 🔥 Ember

Element Edge gives +2. Higher Clash Total wins the lane; equal totals draw.

Vanguard +1

Commit the Vanguard in Lane 1.

Link +1

Commit the Link in Lane 2 or 3 after a card of a different element.

Finisher +1

Make it your final card in a 2–3 card formation, with an opposing card in its lane.

Roles do not restrict placement. A card that misses its condition still clashes normally; it simply receives no Role +1.

4

Count Round Points

Won lane = 2. Extra card with no opponent = 1. More Round Points wins; equal points draw.

5

Claim and continue

Claim a trophy after winning. Other committed cards are discarded, hands refill to 6, and the discard pile reshuffles when needed. Previous Rounds History records the completed round.

THE WHISKERKEEP RULEBOOK

How to Play Project: Prowl

The complete rules of an elemental duel, from reading your first card to claiming the sixth trophy.

🏆

Victory at a glance

Collect two Ember, two Gust, and two Tide trophies before Professor Paws.

🔥2 Ember 🍃2 Gust 💧2 Tide

The Rounds Won display records your victories, but it does not complete the match. Your six elemental trophy slots do.

1

Objective and match structure

  • A match is a series of rounds against Professor Paws.
  • Winning a round awards exactly one trophy. A drawn round awards none.
  • Win the match by collecting two Ember, two Gust, and two Tide trophies.
  • The Rounds Won score is a match record only. It is not a separate win condition.
2

Deck, hand, and difficulty

The shared draw pile contains 39 cards: two copies of every Common and Uncommon card, and one copy of every Rare, Epic, and Legendary card. Both duelists draw up to six cards.

Guided
Shows every planned card's element and power range.
Instinct
Hides his current cards and formation size, but reveals one motive, one formation habit, and one commitment habit that persist for the match.
Blind
Hides his current cards, formation size, and habits. Infer his persistent habits from Previous Rounds History.

Every difficulty includes Previous Rounds History, which records both revealed formations, lane results, Round Points, trophy context, and the claimed trophy after a round is complete. Habits are tendencies, not guarantees. Difficulty changes available information, never the scoring rules or card strength.

3

Read every part of a card

PowerThe printed number and starting value of its Clash Total.

ElementEmber, Gust, or Tide. It determines Element Edge and the trophy's element.

Formation RoleVanguard, Link, or Finisher. Meeting its condition adds Role +1.

RarityCommon through Legendary. It changes the card frame and how many copies enter the shared deck, but never the clash rules or scoring.

Move and loreCharacter flavor only. Cards have no unlisted abilities or hidden effects.

🔥 Emberbeats 🍃 Gustbeats 💧 Tidebeats 🔥 Ember
4

Play a round from start to finish

  1. Read. Study the live clues, visible habits, or Previous Rounds History available in the chosen difficulty.
  2. Build. Drag or click 1–3 cards into consecutive lanes. The first card enters Lane 1.
  3. Order. Return cards to your hand or rearrange them until the formation works as intended.
  4. Preview. Review known bonuses and totals. Hidden information remains unknown until the clash.
  5. Commit. Lock the formation. Every committed card is spent this round.
  6. Resolve. Score each contested lane, then calculate Round Points for the whole formation.
  7. Claim and review. Award a trophy if someone won, inspect the aftermath, then press Next Round.

Lane matching is direct: Lane 1 fights Lane 1, Lane 2 fights Lane 2, and Lane 3 fights Lane 3. This is not one combined 3v3 battle.

5

Calculate a card's Clash Total

Power + Element Edge + Role = Clash Total

PowerBegin with the printed number.

Element Edge +2Add 2 when the card's element beats the opposing card's element.

Role +1Add 1 when the card meets its Formation Role condition.

Lane resultHigher Clash Total wins. Equal Clash Totals draw that lane.

Element Edge is a bonus, not an automatic win. A high-Power card can still defeat a card that has Element Edge.

6

Formation Roles

Vanguard +1

Activate it by committing the Vanguard in Lane 1.

Link +1

Activate it in Lane 2 or 3 when the card committed directly before the Link has a different element from the Link card.

Finisher +1

Activate it when the Finisher is your last committed card in a 2–3 card formation and an opposing card is in that lane.

Roles never restrict placement. You may commit any card in any available lane. If its Role condition is not met, the card still clashes normally but receives no Role +1.

7

Calculate Round Points

Clash Total decides each contested lane. Round Points decide who wins the round.

Number of won lanes× 2 Round Points + Number of extra cards× 1 Round Point = Round Points
  1. Resolve contested lanes A contested lane has one card from each side. Each won lane earns 2 Round Points. Drawn and lost lanes earn 0.
  2. Count extra cards An extra card is a committed card with no opposing card in its lane. Each extra card adds 1 Round Point.
  3. Compare the totals Add all Round Points. More Round Points wins the round. Equal Round Points produce a draw and no trophy.

Common outcomes

1 card vs 1 card 2–0 · Win

Your card wins the only contested lane. There are no extra cards.

1 card vs 2 cards 2–1 · Win

Your card wins its lane for 2. The opponent's extra card adds 1.

1 card vs 3 cards 2–2 · Draw

Your card wins its lane for 2. The opponent's two extra cards also add 2.

2 cards vs 1 card 1–0 · Win

The contested lane draws. Your extra card adds the round's only point.

Keep the two scores separate: an extra card adds 1 Round Point, but it never clashes and never changes Power, Element Edge, Role, or Clash Total.

8

Award the trophy

  • If your won lanes decide the round, claim one of your lane-winning cards.
  • If you have several lane-winning cards, you choose which becomes the trophy. If there is only one, it is claimed automatically.
  • If points from extra cards decide the round, your first extra card becomes the trophy automatically.
  • A drawn round awards no trophy.
9

Discard, draw, and continue

  • The trophy leaves the deck and enters the winner's collection.
  • Every other committed card enters the discard pile.
  • Unplayed cards remain in hand.
  • Each hand refills toward six cards before the next round.
  • When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled back into it.
10

End the match

Required collection2 Ember + 2 Gust + 2 Tide trophies.

Extra trophiesA third trophy of one element is allowed, but it cannot replace a missing element.

Professor PawsHe wins under exactly the same trophy condition.

Rounds WonThis display tracks round results only and never replaces the trophy goal.

11

Subtle strategy

Choose size with a purpose.One card conserves resources. More cards cover more lanes and may score as extra cards, but every committed card is spent.

Order before committing.Formation order determines lanes and can activate or disable Vanguard, Link, and Finisher bonuses.

Plan the trophy element.When several cards win lanes, arrange to give yourself a useful trophy choice. For an extra-card win, the first extra card is fixed.

Respect printed Power.Element Edge helps, but a two-point bonus does not always overcome a much stronger card.

Read hidden information.In Guided, use the live plan clues. In Instinct, combine all three visible habits. In Blind, compare several entries in Previous Rounds History to infer those hidden habits yourself.

Track the real goal.Do not spend valuable cards collecting a third trophy of an element when another element is still missing.
